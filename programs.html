What we run, in detail

Four ways to learn, all with materials included and no experience assumed.

Program 01 · Starting from nothing

Adult classes

Beginner courses start where beginners actually are — how to hold a brush, how to mix a colour that is not mud, how to look at something before drawing it. Nobody is asked to show previous work, and the room is full of other people who have never done this either.

  • 6 to 8 weeks
  • Adults
  • Per course
  • Nothing to bring

Program 02 · Screened and consented

Children's classes & camps

Children's classes use materials labelled non-toxic and appropriate to the age group, and groups are kept small enough that every child gets attention. Instructors working with under-18s are background screened and a parent or guardian completes consent and emergency contact details before a child attends.

  • Weekly or per camp
  • Children
  • Per term or week
  • Parental consent

Program 03 · One technique, one day

Weekend workshops

A good way to find out whether you enjoy something before committing to eight weeks of it. One technique, one day, everything provided — and popular as a thing to do with a friend who has also been meaning to try it for years.

  • Adults and teens
  • Per workshop
  • Nothing to bring
